Ruitong Tian

PhD student · University of Florida

I am a PhD student at the University of Florida, advised by Eric Jing Du in the ICIC Lab. I work on robot learning for dexterous manipulation, connecting wearable human demonstrations, tactile data, and learned representations and policies.

My research asks how we can capture useful contact information from human manipulation and carry it into robot learning. ExoBridge studies how bare-hand video can predict measured motion and tactile state. ExoComfort studies how wearable-interface design and tactile representations affect demonstrations and learned manipulation policies.

Under submission · 2026 · Robot learning

ExoBridge: Learning a Bare Hand to Hand-Worn Exoskeleton Mapping through Human Limb Coupling

Ruitong Tian, Xianyao Li, Noah B. Wilson, Fang Xu, and Eric Jing Du

Paired bimanual demonstrations connect bare-hand RGB video with the motion and tactile state of a sensorized exoskeleton.

1,215 demonstrations across four tasks · 0.916 contact AUROC on held-out collection sessions.

Paper ↗PDF ↗Research figure ↗
Research details

A temporal visual model predicts contact, tactile intensity, and relative encoder motion from video alone. Methods include MediaPipe hand crops, frozen DINOv2 visual features, and causal temporal convolutions. Evaluation separates collection sessions across four manipulation tasks.

From Wearable Interfaces to Dexterous Policies: Contact Shifts and Tactile Representations

Ruitong Tian, Fang Xu, Noah B. Wilson, Xianyao Li, and Eric Jing Du

Wearable-interface geometry shapes the tactile interactions captured in human demonstrations; tactile representations affect learned manipulation policies.

12-DoF XHand + Franka Emika · pooled policy success 35% → 52% with richer tactile inputs.

Paper ↗PDF ↗Research figure ↗
Research details

Methods include wearable demonstration collection, tactile representation comparison, and Diffusion Policy. The reported preprint comparison uses binary aggregate versus spatial-plus-force tactile inputs. Both deploy on a 12-DoF XHand mounted on a Franka Emika arm.

First-author work

Ergonomics · Accepted October 9, 2026

Semantically Enhanced Eye-Tracking Controls in Human-Robot Interaction: A Comparison Study

Ruitong Tian, Hengxu You, Fang Xu, Jiahao Wu, Bowen Sun, Thanakon Uthai, and Eric Jing Du

A 30-participant within-subject comparison of gaze, voice, and hand-gesture control of a Franka Emika arm with HoloLens 2.

Mean completion time per step: 17.10 s with gaze versus 32.78 s with hand gesture.

Research details & contributions

The semantic gaze and voice interfaces combined FastSAM segmentation with Azure Kinect RGB-D target localization. I contributed to methodology, investigation, data curation, formal analysis, and writing. Evaluation included task performance, gaze behavior, and NASA-TLX workload measures.

Under submission · 2026 · Tactile navigation

Nudge Before You Push: Physics-Aware Navigation via Tactile Probing

Xianyao Li, Fang Xu, Ruitong Tian, Bowen Sun, Xiao Hu, Yang Ye, and Jing Du

TANav briefly nudges obstructing containers and uses TacPhys to read force sequences, estimate container mass, and inform push-or-detour decisions.

Simulation: missed pushes 28.7% → 5.5%, comparing mean peak force with TacPhys at comparable low-risk operating points.

Paper ↗PDF ↗Research figure ↗
Research details

Repeated-patrol planning is evaluated in simulation. Hardware studies separately assess offline mass transfer and calibration: the calibrated boundary yields 93.5% pooled offline accuracy, while a distinct raw-peak rule gives 15 of 20 correct online decisions on unseen boxes.
